# Demo asset sources

The guest-mode Library photographs under `public/assets/guest-library/` were downloaded from the image URLs previously used directly by `src/lib/guestData.js`. They are bundled locally so the guest showcase remains deterministic and does not fail when a hotlinked image is removed or slow.

The photographs are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), which permits free commercial and non-commercial use. They remain guest/demo presentation assets only; production capture media is user-provided and served through the authenticated private-media path.
